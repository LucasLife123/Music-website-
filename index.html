INTRODUCTION

What Are Musical Instruments?

Musical instruments are objects designed to produce sound for musical expression. They create sound through vibrating strings, moving air, striking surfaces, or electronic signals.

Music connects people across cultures and generations. From ancient drums to modern synthesizers, instruments continue to evolve and inspire musicians around the world.
